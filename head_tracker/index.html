Keep head centered

Servo 1 = horizontal pan · Servo 2 = vertical tilt. Pan uses the full supported range, 0–1000. Tilt uses its taught setpoints A/B above. Low-latency tracking uses a smoothed face center, a center dead zone, and gradual speed changes. Speed limit: 400 units/sec (about 96°/sec); default: 180 units/sec. When a face leaves view, tracking predicts its motion briefly, then slows to a hold. Camera failure or a disconnected browser still stops movement. Tracking and sweep run separately.

If an axis moves away from your head, stop tracking, reverse that axis, and retry at low speed.
