1 Floor not run

Take the robots off the floor. Lay the two floor tags flat, the same distance in front of the camera, one to each side. Measure along the floor from directly below the lens.

Advanced: tag size and IDs, robot rear-tag offset

2 Robots not run

  1. Put both robots back on the floor, in view, about 1–2.5 m from the camera and at least 0.5 m apart.
  2. Face each chassis and head straight forward, away from the camera (rear tags toward the camera). Robot 2's current head angle becomes "forward".
  3. Keep everything still and out of the way while it runs (about a minute). Robot 2's head will look around; the wheels never move.

3 Usable space (walls) not set

How wide the space the robots may use is, measured across it at the camera (wall to wall, or between any obstacles). The camera is assumed to be in the middle, so the walls are half that width to each side. Robots keep half their body plus the clearance away from each wall. No robot moves.
